# Data & Asset Credits

- Planet textures: [Solar System Scope](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/) — CC Attribution 4.0.
- Planet/Moon/Sun positions: [astronomy-engine](https://github.com/cosinekitty/astronomy) — MIT.
- Star catalog (bootstrap): [HYG Database](https://github.com/astronexus/HYG-Database) — CC0.
- Star catalog (full): ESA [Gaia DR3](https://gea.esac.esa.int/archive/); this work has made use of data from the European Space Agency (ESA) mission Gaia, processed by the Gaia Data Processing and Analysis Consortium (DPAC).
- Galaxy catalog (deep/wedge): Sloan Digital Sky Survey (SDSS), via [SkyServer](https://skyserver.sdss.org/). Funding for the Sloan Digital Sky Survey has been provided by the Alfred P. Sloan Foundation, the U.S. Department of Energy Office of Science, and the Participating Institutions.
- Galaxy catalog (all-sky, local): 2MASS Redshift Survey (2MRS) — Huchra, J. P., et al. 2012, ApJS, 199, 26 (VizieR catalog [J/ApJS/199/26](https://vizier.cds.unistra.fr/viz-bin/VizieR?-source=J/ApJS/199/26)).
- Milky Way layers: **model layers**, not individually-real stars. The interior layer's sky-plane density is real — sampled from 1M Gaia DR3 sky positions (uniform `random_index` shuffle) — with modeled depth (exponential disk Rd≈2.6 kpc, hz≈0.3 kpc + 4-arm log spiral + dust + bulge). The exterior layer (shown from ~20 kpc out) is sampled entirely from that analytic model, with no Gaia positions. Both built by `scripts/build-milkyway.ts`; see README "The deep field" for the honesty note.
- Near-field interstellar dust (within 1.25 kpc of the Sun): Edenhofer, G., Zucker, C., Frank, P., Saydjari, A. K., Speagle, J. S., Finkbeiner, D., &amp; Enßlin, T. 2024, A&amp;A, 685, A82 — "A parsec-scale Galactic 3D dust map out to 1.25 kpc from the Sun". Data product on [Zenodo record 8187943](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.8187943), file `mean_and_std_healpix.fits` (posterior mean, HEALPix nside=256 NESTED x 516 log-spaced radii, differential extinction in units of *E* of Zhang, Green &amp; Rix 2023 per parsec). `scripts/build-dustmap.ts` integrates it along distance into a cumulative-extinction grid cached (not committed) at `scripts/cache/edenhofer-cum.bin`; `scripts/build-milkyway.ts` uses it for the sight-line optical depth inside 1.25 kpc, handing over to the analytic dust model beyond. Not a redistribution of the map itself.
- Stellar effective temperatures: Gaia DR3 `teff_gspphot` (GSP-Phot Aeneas, Andrae, R., et al. 2023, A&amp;A, 674, A27), used to set star colors where available in place of the BP−RP photometric index.
- Real imagery for curated galaxies and deep-sky objects: [hips2fits](https://alasky.cds.unistra.fr/hips-image-services/hips2fits) cutout service, survey `CDS/P/DSS2/color` — Centre de Données astronomiques de Strasbourg (CDS). Digitized Sky Survey 2 data via CDS's HiPS infrastructure.
- Deep-sky object catalog (~12.4k NGC/IC objects): [OpenNGC](https://github.com/mattiaverga/OpenNGC) — Mattia Verga, CC-BY-SA-4.0.
- Constellation lines (sky view): [d3-celestial](https://github.com/ofrohn/d3-celestial) `constellations.lines.json` — Olaf Frohn, BSD-3-Clause license.
- Asteroid orbits: [MPCORB](https://minorplanetcenter.net/iau/MPCORB.html), the Minor Planet Center Orbit Database — IAU Minor Planet Center / Center for Astrophysics | Harvard & Smithsonian. `public/asteroids.bin` is a derived subset (~486k objects with H &lt; 17, nine orbital-element columns) built by `scripts/build-asteroids.ts`, not a redistribution of MPCORB itself. The H &lt; 17 cut is chosen for the belt's visual density, not as a completeness limit — MPCORB is far from complete at that magnitude. Positions are propagated with an unperturbed two-body Kepler solver (`src/sim/kepler.ts`) from each object's osculating elements — accurate to ~1,500 km for Ceres against JPL Horizons near the elements' epoch, and degrading the further sim time runs from it.
